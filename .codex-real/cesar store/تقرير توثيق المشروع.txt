   ↓
POST /api/products
   ↓
Validate Input
   ↓
Normalize Images
   ↓
Check Duplicate
   ↓
Insert into DB
   ↓
Return Response
   ↓
UI Update
🖼️ Image Lifecycle Flow
Upload Image
   ↓
Normalize URL
   ↓
Store in DB
   ↓
Link to Product
   ↓
[Update/Delete]
   ↓
Compare Usage
   ↓

   ↓

   ↓

   ↓

   ↓

   ↓
Extract Paths
   ↓

   ↓
