# Cesar Store Keyword Target Map

Last updated: 2026-07-17

Purpose: connect each important search phrase to one clear page so Google, Bing, and answer engines can understand which Cesar Store page should rank for each intent.

Important note: keyword research tools do not push rankings by themselves. Ranking improves when the matching page is useful, indexable, internally linked, trusted, and consistently cited with the same brand/entity signals.

## Priority model

- P1: protect and expand brand visibility. These terms should become the easiest wins.
- P2: category and commercial-intent searches. These need strong guide pages and internal links to products.
- P3: specific product-use searches. These depend on product pages, descriptions, images, stock, and Merchant/feed health.
- P4: monitoring and iteration. These are reviewed after impressions start appearing.

## Measurement checklist

Review weekly after deployment and indexing:

- Google Search Console > Performance > Queries:
  - Brand aliases: متجر سيزر, سيزر ستور, سيزر شوب, Cesar Store, Cesar Shop.
  - Category terms: منظفات سيارات, معطرات سيارات, اكسسوارات سيارات, كمبريسور سيارة, مساحات سيارة.
- Google Search Console > Pages:
  - Check that guide pages are indexed.
  - Check that important product pages are discovered and indexed.
- Bing Webmaster Tools:
  - Check URL Inspection for the home page and guide pages.
  - Check Keyword Research for impressions on brand and category terms.
  - Use IndexNow after substantial product or guide-page updates.
- Merchant Center:
  - Keep product feed healthy, but do not manually edit products inside Merchant Center.
